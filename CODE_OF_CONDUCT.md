# Code of Conduct

VoidRemove is intended to be a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and constructive; disagree with ideas, not people.
- Welcome newcomers and help them learn the codebase.
- Accept feedback gracefully and assume good intent.
- Respect that this tool touches real systems: safety-first decisions are non-negotiable.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Trolling, derogatory comments, and inflammatory remarks.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Any conduct that would make a reasonable person feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to all project spaces: repositories, issues, discussions, and any official
channel. Report problems to the maintainers through a private channel or
[Security policy](SECURITY.md). Reports are taken seriously and handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or sanction behaviour that violates this code.

## A note on safety culture

Because VoidRemove removes software from operating systems, reviewers will hold a higher
bar for changes that delete, quarantine, or modify system state. Expect careful scrutiny —
it comes from care for users, not distrust of you.
